The Amazon Sales Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence dashboard developed entirely in Microsoft Excel to analyze Amazon sales performance across multiple business dimensions. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights, enabling users to monitor sales trends, customer activity, product performance, delivery efficiency, and payment preferences.

Designed with interactive Month and Year slicers, the dashboard allows users to explore sales data dynamically for 2023 and 2024, making it easier to compare performance over time and support data-driven decision-making.


🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was to create a centralized and interactive reporting dashboard that helps stakeholders monitor key sales metrics and identify business trends without manually analyzing large datasets.

The dashboard answers important business questions such as:

How many customers placed orders?
What is the total sales revenue?
How many products were sold?
What is the cancellation and return rate?
Which brands generate the highest sales?
Which product categories perform the best?
Which countries contribute the highest revenue?
Which payment methods are most preferred?
What percentage of orders were delivered successfully?

📈 Key Insights
The business generated over 1.62 million in total sales.
A 74% delivery rate indicates that most customer orders were completed successfully.
The United States contributes the highest sales among all countries.
Categories such as Books, Sports & Outdoors, and Toys & Games are among the top revenue-generating segments.
Several brands consistently outperform others, highlighting strong brand demand.
Credit Card and Debit Card are the most commonly used payment methods, followed by UPI and Amazon Pay.
The cancellation and return rates remain relatively low, indicating efficient order fulfillment and customer satisfaction.

💡 Business Value

This dashboard enables businesses to monitor sales performance in real time, identify high-performing products and brands, analyze customer purchasing behavior, evaluate delivery efficiency, and optimize sales strategies through interactive reporting. By consolidating multiple business metrics into a single dashboard, it simplifies decision-making and improves overall reporting efficiency.
